Privacy policy

Korbin Bot is a private, family-run assistant used only by the family's own Google accounts.

What it accesses

  • Google Chat: it reads and replies to messages in the one conversation it is set up for.
  • Google Drive: it uploads requested music and videos to the family's own folders and checks what is already there.
  • Your Google account email address, only to confirm the correct account signed in.

How the data is used

Data is used only to answer requests and deliver files to the family's own Drive. It is stored on the family's own computer, is not sold or shared with anyone, and is not used for advertising.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time from your Google Account under Security, then Third-party apps and services.

Contact

Use the support email shown on the Google sign-in screen for this app.
